# Catalyst Solo AI Hackathon — 3-Minute Pitch Script

**Project:** AgriYield Voice Slice — "Bolo, Dekho, Jaano" (Speak, See, Know)
**Format:** 3 minutes, spoken live over the deployed demo. Rehearse at least twice in Hour 6, timed, against the live URL — never against localhost.
**Timing:** 0:00–0:30 problem · 0:30–2:00 live demo · 2:00–2:30 how it works · 2:30–3:00 what's next.
**Rule:** if the live demo fails at any point, say so out loud, switch to the typed path, and keep going. Never fake a turn.

---

## Full script (approx. 380 words — ~3 minutes at a calm pace)

### 0:00–0:30 — The problem (30 s)

> "Pakistan's smallholder farmers feed the country, but most of them cannot use a farming app — because the app asks them to type, in a script they rarely write in. The knowledge exists: weather forecasts, soil data, market prices. The farmer just can't reach it. AgriYield Voice fixes the last step. A farmer speaks a question in Urdu — or shows a photo of a sick crop — and gets one clear answer back, spoken aloud, in Urdu."

### 0:30–2:00 — The live demo (90 s)

> *[Tap the microphone. Speak clearly, slowly, in Urdu:]*
> "Gandum ki fasal ko is haftay kitna pani chahiye?"
>
> *[Point at the transcript appearing on screen.]*
> "You can see exactly what it heard. One tap —"
>
> *[Tap "Jawab Suno". Let the spoken answer play. While it plays, point at the screen:]*
> "The answer comes back spoken and written: how much water this week, the seven-day weather behind it, and the soil fact behind it — every fact labelled with its source. Nothing here is a guess: live data is labelled live, and market prices are labelled as dated references, never presented as today's price."
>
> *[Turn 2 — only if Turn 1 worked. Hold up / upload the photo.]*
> "And if the problem is something you can see — yellowing wheat leaves — one photo gives one answer: what it most likely is, what to check next, and honestly, what a photo alone cannot confirm."

### 2:00–2:30 — How it works (30 s)

> "Underneath, this is AgriYield Advisor — already live, already serving farmers — with one new layer: Urdu voice in, Urdu voice out, and a photo path. The grounding is real data: Open-Meteo weather for 25 Punjab districts, ISRIC soil data, dated market references, and a yield model backtested on twenty-four years of real data. When the AI is unsure, a labelled deterministic fallback answers instead of inventing one — in farming, a wrong confident answer is worse than no answer."

### 2:30–3:00 — What's next (30 s)

> "Today this is one question and one photo. Next is the whole season: sowing plans spoken aloud, voice reminders before irrigation days, and the same voice door on every crop we already cover. The farmer never has to learn to type. Thank you — the live demo is at this URL, and I'm happy to take your questions."

## If something goes wrong live (say these exact lines)

- **Voice recognition fails / hears nothing:** "Voice input didn't catch that on this device — the typed path gives the same answer, so let me type the same question." *(Type it, continue.)*
- **Spoken answer doesn't play:** "The audio didn't play, but the written answer is the same one — here it is, with its sources." *(Point at the screen, continue.)*
- **Photo turn fails:** "The photo path needs the live AI call and it isn't responding right now — the voice turn you just saw runs on the fallback alone, so the core demo stands. The photo turn is documented as the second step."
- **Whole page fails to load:** "The live link is waking up — while it loads, here is the recorded demo video of the same two turns on the same URL." *(Play the video; do not improvise a second demo.)*

## Rehearsal checklist (Hour 6)

- [ ] Timed twice, both under 3:00.
- [ ] Exact Urdu question rehearsed out loud (not just read) — voice recognition tested with your actual voice, on the actual demo device.
- [ ] Photo chosen in advance and tested once end to end.
- [ ] Typed backup question ready (same question, pasted, one tap).
- [ ] Demo video recorded and uploaded as the fallback artefact.
- [ ] Live URL opened cold on a second browser/device before the pitch.
